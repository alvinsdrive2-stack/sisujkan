# LSP Gatensi — Sistem Uji Kompetensi (SISUJ)

## Product Purpose

Digital platform for managing the full lifecycle of professional competency certification (uji kompetensi) in Indonesia. Covers candidate onboarding, document submission, assessment scheduling, online examinations across multiple instruments, and certificate signing workflow.

Regulated context: BNSP-governed framework. Every form has a code (FR.APL.01, FR.AK.01, etc.) and must comply with LSP assessment standards. The system is not a generic LMS — it's a compliance-critical records and workflow tool.

## Users

**Asesi (Certification Candidates)**
- Fill registration forms (APL.01, APL.02)
- Submit supporting documents
- Take structured assessments (observasi, wawancara, portofolio, ujian tertulis)
- Track certification status in real-time

**Asesor (Assessors/Evaluators)**
- Review candidate submissions
- Conduct and score assessments using standardized FR instruments
- Sign off on assessment results
- Access documents via secure viewer

**Admin LSP (Certification Body Admin)**
- Manage certification schemes and schedules
- Oversee all candidates and assessment sessions
- Generate reports, manage user accounts

**Admin TUK (Testing Unit Admin)**
- Manage attendance at assessment venues
- Coordinate logistics, upload candidate photos
- Record assessment activities (kegiatan)

**Manajer (Manager)**
- Operations oversight, review assessment data
- Schedule coordination

**Direktur (Director)**
- Final signing authority on certification documents (SK)
- Reviews and countersigns digitally

**Komtek (Technical Committee)**
- Review and validate assessment documents
- Edit schedules, audit document completeness

## Brand

LSP Gatensi — a professional competency certification body. Institutional, credible, precise. Indonesian government-adjacent authority. Visual identity uses deep navy blue with a red accent (referencing the Indonesian flag colors: merah-putih). Not a startup. Not a consumer product. This should feel like a serious professional tool that also respects the user's time.

## Tone

Formal but clear. Indonesian language throughout. No jargon beyond what the BNSP framework requires. Forms are structured and labeled with official codes. Error messages should be helpful, not bureaucratic.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS dashboards with decorative stat cards
- Cookie-cutter government portal aesthetics (Bootstrap, heavy navbars, rainbow status badges)
- Startup landing-page design language applied to internal tools
- AI-generated "clean admin" templates (shadcn defaults, hero metrics, identical card grids)

## Strategic Principles

1. **Compliance over cleverness** — The FR form codes and document flow are fixed by regulation. Don't redesign them; make them easier to complete.
2. **Role clarity** — Each role sees only what they need. Navigation and UI should reinforce role context.
3. **Trust signals** — Institutional weight matters. The design should feel authoritative, not playful.
4. **Document-first** — Most critical flows are document workflows. PDFs, signatures, form submissions. Design around documents.
